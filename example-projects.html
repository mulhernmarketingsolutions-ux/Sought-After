Real Numbers, Real Scope

Three real shapes a project can take.

These aren't fixed packages off a menu — every project gets scoped individually on a call, based on what you actually need. But if you want an honest feel for what different investment levels tend to include, here's a real breakdown.

A Focused Fix Around $1,000

Identity Only

For when one specific piece is actually the problem — not the whole brand. Everything else already works; the look just hasn't caught up.

  • A new logo & mark, built from scratch
  • A tightened color & font system
  • Simple usage guide so it stays consistent everywhere

Best for: a business with everything else already working.

Most Common
The Sweet Spot Around $3,500

Identity + Website

The most common starting point — a full brand identity that finally looks like you, and a site to put it on.

  • Full brand identity — logo, fonts, colors, voice
  • Custom website, built & launched
  • Bio & caption scripts to launch with

Best for: a real business that's outgrown its current look.

Go All In Around $8,500

The Full Build

For a business ready to invest in the whole system — not just the look, but how it actually brings in clients.

  • Full brand identity, built from scratch
  • Custom website, built & launched
  • Lead-gen strategy & a built client-acquisition system
  • Weekly calls throughout the build
  • Social starter kit & ongoing content calendar

Best for: starting from zero, or ready to go all in.

Every deal looks a little different once we actually talk through it — these are ranges, not quotes. That conversation is free.
